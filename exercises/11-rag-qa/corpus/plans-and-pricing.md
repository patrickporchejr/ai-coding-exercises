# Plans and pricing

Tidepool offers three plans. All prices are in US dollars.

## Free
Up to 10 team members and one schedule. Includes shift scheduling, the mobile app, and email notifications. No integrations and no API access.

## Team
$8 per user per month, billed monthly. Unlimited schedules, shift swaps, time-off tracking, the Slack and Google Calendar integrations, and API access.

## Business
$15 per user per month, billed monthly. Everything in Team, plus single sign-on (SAML), SCIM provisioning, EU data residency, audit logs, and a 99.9% uptime SLA.

## Annual billing
Paying annually gives a 20% discount on Team and Business.

## Changing plans
Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period.
