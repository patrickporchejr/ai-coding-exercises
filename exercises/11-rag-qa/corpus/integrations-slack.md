# Slack integration

The Slack integration is available on the Team and Business plans.

It sends shift reminders, swap requests, and schedule changes to Slack, and adds a `/tidepool` command for checking your next shift.

A Slack workspace admin must approve the Tidepool app before it can be installed. In Tidepool, go to **Settings > Integrations > Slack** and choose **Connect**.

Each Tidepool workspace can connect to one Slack workspace.
