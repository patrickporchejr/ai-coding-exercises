# Mobile app

The Tidepool app is available for **iOS 16 or later** and **Android 11 or later**.

Members can view schedules, request swaps and time off, and clock in and out. Managers can also approve requests from the app.

## Offline mode
The app caches your schedule for the next 14 days. Without a connection you can **view** your schedule, but you can't make changes, request swaps, or clock in until you're back online.
