# Data retention and residency

## Deleted workspaces
When a workspace is deleted, it's kept in a recoverable state for **30 days**. A workspace owner can restore it during that time by contacting support. After 30 days, it's permanently purged.

## Backups
Encrypted backups are kept for 90 days. Backups are for disaster recovery only and can't be used to restore individual records.

## EU data residency
On the Business plan, you can choose to store your workspace's data in the EU. EU data is hosted in **Frankfurt, Germany**. Choose the region when you create the workspace; it can't be changed later.
