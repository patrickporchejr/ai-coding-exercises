# Time off and accrual

Admins create time-off policies under **Settings > Time off**. Each policy sets how many hours members accrue per hour worked, or a fixed yearly allowance.

## Carryover
Unused time off carries over to the next year up to a **maximum of 40 hours**. Anything above 40 hours is forfeited on January 1 unless an admin raises the cap for that policy.

## Requests
Members request time off from the mobile app or the web. Requests for dates that already have a published shift need manager approval and must be submitted at least 7 days in advance.
