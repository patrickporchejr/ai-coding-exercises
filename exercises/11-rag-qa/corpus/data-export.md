# Exporting your data

Only **workspace admins** can export data.

You can export schedules as CSV or as calendar files (ICS) from **Settings > Data > Export**. Large exports are prepared in the background, and you get an email with a download link when they're ready.

Export download links **expire after 7 days**. After that, start a new export.

For automated exports, use the API's `/exports` endpoint (Team and Business plans).
