# Team plan price change

Starting **July 1, 2026**, the Team plan costs **$10 per user per month** for new customers. Business plan pricing does not change.

Existing Team customers keep their current price of $8 per user per month until **January 1, 2027**, after which they move to the new price at their next renewal.

The annual billing discount stays at 20%.
