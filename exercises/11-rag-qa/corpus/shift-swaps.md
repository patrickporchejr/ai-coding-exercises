# Shift swaps

Team members can offer a shift to a specific coworker or post it for anyone eligible to pick up.

## Approval
By default, a manager must approve every swap. Managers can turn on **auto-approve** for a schedule so swaps between eligible members go through immediately.

Even with auto-approve on, any swap for a shift that starts **within 48 hours** always needs manager approval.

## Eligibility
A member can only pick up a shift if they have the required role for it and the swap doesn't push them over their weekly hour limit.
