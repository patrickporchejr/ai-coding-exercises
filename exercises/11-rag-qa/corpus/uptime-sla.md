# Uptime SLA

The Business plan includes a **99.9% monthly uptime** commitment. Free and Team plans have no SLA.

If monthly uptime falls below 99.9%, Business customers receive a service credit of **10% of that month's fee for each full 0.1% below the target**, up to a maximum of 50%.

Credits must be requested within 30 days of the end of the affected month by contacting support. Scheduled maintenance announced at least 48 hours ahead doesn't count as downtime.
