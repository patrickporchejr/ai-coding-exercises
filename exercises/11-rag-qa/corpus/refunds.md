# Refund policy

**Monthly plans** are not refundable. If you cancel, you keep access until the end of the billing period you've paid for.

**Annual plans** can be refunded on a prorated basis if you cancel within 30 days of the start or renewal of your annual term. After 30 days, annual plans are not refundable.

Refunds go back to the original payment method and take 5 to 10 business days to appear. To request one, a workspace owner or billing admin contacts support from **Settings > Billing**.
