# Deleting your account or workspace

## Deleting your own account
Any member can delete their own account from **Profile > Account > Delete account**. Your shifts stay in the workspace's history, shown as "Former member."

## If you're the workspace owner
A workspace owner can't delete their account while they still own a workspace. First either **transfer ownership** to another admin (**Settings > Members**) or **delete the workspace**.

## Deleting a workspace
Only the owner can delete a workspace, from **Settings > General > Delete workspace**. Paid subscriptions are canceled at the same time. See the data retention article for how long deleted workspaces can be recovered.
