# Google Calendar sync

Tidepool can sync shifts with Google Calendar in both directions. Sync runs **every 5 minutes**.

Shifts appear in Google Calendar as events. Busy events in Google Calendar show up in Tidepool as unavailability.

## Conflicts
If a shift is edited in both places between syncs, **Tidepool's version wins** and the Google Calendar event is overwritten.

To connect, each member goes to **Profile > Calendar** and signs in with Google. Admins can't connect calendars on someone else's behalf.
